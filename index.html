WELCOME TO MY ACADEMIC HOMEPAGE

Welcome! 欢迎来到我的学术主页

I am an Associate Professor at Shenzhen Tourism College, Jinan University.

My research interests include service robots, human–robot interaction, and organizational behavior in hospitality and tourism. I am interested in understanding how intelligent technologies shape service experiences, employee behaviors, and human–technology relationships.

我现任暨南大学深圳旅游学院副教授，主要研究方向包括服务机器人、人机交互，以及酒店与旅游业组织行为。我关注智能技术如何影响服务体验、员工行为及人与技术之间的关系。

RESEARCH INTERESTS 研究兴趣

  • Service Robot
  • Human–Robot Interaction
  • Organizational Behavior in Hospitality and Tourism
