Research 研究方向

RESEARCH INTERESTS 研究兴趣

My research interests lie at the intersection of service management, hospitality and tourism, human–robot interaction, and organizational behavior. I am particularly interested in understanding how emerging technologies reshape service encounters, employee experiences, and value creation in hospitality and tourism contexts.

研究兴趣主要围绕服务管理、旅游与接待业、人机交互以及组织行为展开，重点关注新兴技术如何影响服务互动、员工体验，以及旅游与接待业中的价值创造。

SERVICE ROBOTS AND HUMAN–ROBOT INTERACTION 服务机器人与人机交互

This research stream focuses on the interaction and collaboration between people and service robots in hospitality and tourism settings. Topics include robot roles and capabilities, human–robot collaboration, employee–robot co-work, customer responses to robotic services, and the ways in which human–robot interaction may facilitate or constrain service value co-creation.

该方向关注旅游与接待服务场景中的人机互动与协作，涉及服务机器人的角色与能力、人机协作模式、员工与机器人的共同工作、顾客对机器人服务的反应，以及人机互动对服务价值共创的影响。

  • Service robot roles, capabilities, and proactivity
  • Human–robot collaboration and employee–robot co-work
  • Customer responses and value co-creation in robotic services
  • Human–robot interaction in hospitality and tourism

HOSPITALITY ORGANIZATIONAL BEHAVIOR AND HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT 接待业组织行为与人力资源管理

This stream examines employees’ experiences and behaviors in hospitality and tourism organizations, especially as service work is transformed by intelligent technologies. Relevant topics include emotional labor, employee well-being, work engagement, psychological empowerment, workplace relationships, leadership, and employee service behavior.

该方向关注旅游与接待业组织中的员工体验与行为，尤其关注智能技术发展背景下服务工作的变化。研究主题包括情绪劳动、员工幸福感、工作投入、心理授权、职场关系、领导风格以及员工服务行为等。

  • Emotional labor and service recovery
  • Employee well-being, loneliness, and psychological empowerment
  • Leadership, workplace relationships, and employee behavior
  • Employee learning, engagement, and proactive service performance

AI, SMART SERVICE, AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION 人工智能、智慧服务与数字化转型

This stream explores the application of artificial intelligence and digital technologies in service and hospitality contexts. Topics reflected in my research projects include AI-enabled service transformation, human–AI collaboration, generative AI hallucinations and user responses, and the organizational implications of AI adoption.

该方向关注人工智能与数字技术在服务及接待业场景中的应用，研究主题包括人工智能驱动的服务转型、人机协同、生成式 AI 幻觉及用户反馈，以及人工智能应用带来的组织管理问题。

DESTINATION MARKETING AND TOURISM EXPERIENCE 目的地营销与旅游体验

This research area concerns technology-enabled tourism experiences, destination and service marketing, and the design and development of tourism offerings. Related interests include innovative technology marketing, immersive cultural tourism, wellness services, and older adults’ tourism.

该方向关注技术赋能的旅游体验、目的地与服务营销，以及旅游产品和服务的设计与发展。相关主题包括创新技术营销、沉浸式文旅、康养服务与老年旅游等。

SERVICE INNOVATION AND VALUE CO-CREATION 服务创新与价值共创

This stream considers how customers, employees, organizations, and intelligent technologies participate in service processes. It includes value co-creation and value co-destruction, service innovation and management, service customization, and the conditions that shape customer and employee experiences.

该方向关注顾客、员工、组织与智能技术如何共同参与服务过程，涉及价值共创与价值共毁、服务创新与管理、服务定制，以及影响顾客和员工体验的相关因素。
