Research Interests

  • Service Robot · 服务机器人
  • Human–Robot Interaction · 人机交互

Research Themes 研究主题

Research themes represented in the provided project materials include human–robot collaboration; service robot roles and employee well-being; emotional labor in human–robot interaction; service failure and recovery; generative AI hallucinations; and customer–robot value co-creation.

根据所提供的科研项目材料，相关研究主题包括人机协作、服务机器人角色与员工幸福感、人机交互中的情绪劳动、服务失误与补救、生成式人工智能幻觉，以及顾客与服务机器人的价值共创。
